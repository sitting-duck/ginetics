01 — Purple

Detoxing & cleansing

Should I detox or cleanse?

Detoxing removes toxins; cleansing resets your diet. Choose based on your health goals — and consult a healthcare provider.

What’s the difference?

Detoxing: Eliminates harmful substances (for example alcohol and pollutants).

Cleansing: Improves digestion and energy through specific diets or juice plans.

Is it safe during pregnancy?

Not without medical supervision. Prioritize a nutrient-rich diet.

What if I’m on medication or have health issues?

Always consult your healthcare provider first.

Can diabetics or people on insulin detox?

Only under a doctor’s guidance to maintain safe blood sugar levels.

When should I start a detox?

Mornings are ideal — but consistency matters most.

What foods are allowed during a detox?

Fruits, vegetables, lean proteins, and seeds. Avoid processed items, caffeine, alcohol, and added sugars.

How much water should I drink?

64–80 oz (8–10 glasses) per day.

Is detoxing safe for kids?

Only under professional supervision.

What if I feel dizzy, nauseous, or shaky?

Stop immediately, hydrate, and contact a health professional.

02 — Green

Juice, lifestyle & diet

What’s in the bottle, and who it’s for.

Can I drink your juices daily?

Yes, in moderation as part of a balanced diet.

Are your juices vegan?

100% plant-based and vegan-friendly.

Any nut content?

Juices are nut-free but may be made in facilities that handle nuts. Always read labels if allergic.

Can I work out while cleansing?

Light to moderate activity is fine — avoid intense workouts if tired.

03 — Citrus

Ordering, shipping & storage

How it gets to your fridge — DFW only.

Where can I buy Ginetics Juice?

Order via our website, Instagram, or local retailers.

Juice shelf life?

Consume after opening within one hour. Juice can remain fresh in the refrigerator 3–5 days. Unopened pouches remain fresh when frozen for up to six months. Do not freeze glass bottles. Check the label.

Calorie content?

Varies by juice. Nutrition info is available on labels or online.

Does order matter when drinking juices?

Not required, but following our guide enhances results.

Are your juices kosher?

Yes — look for the kosher certification symbol.

My juice arrived cold but unfrozen — is it safe?

Yes. Just refrigerate and use by the expiration date.

Do I need to be home for delivery?

Ideally yes. Otherwise, ensure a secure, cool drop-off spot.

Can we pick a delivery time?

We only ship to the DFW area. Unfortunately, we are unable to schedule a specific delivery time; packages may arrive sooner or later than expected. Some delivery companies do not operate on Sundays, so we cannot guarantee packages will arrive on Mondays. The cost of shipping may vary, including options for free shipping specials, 2-day, and overnight delivery via FedEx or Amazon.

How fast is shipping?

3–7 business days based on location.

Storage instructions?

Keep juices sealed and refrigerated. Consume after opening within one hour. Juice can remain fresh in the refrigerator 3–5 days. Unopened pouches remain fresh when frozen for up to six months. Do not freeze glass bottles.

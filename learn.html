Product overview

Please find detailed information regarding our product line and care guidelines below.

Nutritional focus

Our all-natural, non-GMO fruit and vegetable juices contain no preservatives, chemicals, caffeine, or added sugars. Formulated with lemon, lime, and botanicals such as turmeric, ginger, and lemongrass, they offer a flavorful way to support wellness, immunity, and hydration.

Product line & packaging

Products are available in 8 oz and 11 oz sizes, packaged in BPA-free bottles (8 oz) and pouches (11 oz). The pouches are durable, reusable, and suitable for freezing and thawing without sacrificing quality. Please note that glass bottles should not be frozen.

Who it’s for

Our beverages are designed for both individual consumers and commercial outlets. They are suitable for all ages as everyday refreshments, pre- and post-workout drinks, healthy snack replacements, or as part of a wellness detox and balanced diet plan.

Usage & care

Consume products after opening within one hour. Juice can remain fresh in the refrigerator 3 to 5 days. Unopened pouches remain fresh when frozen for up to six months. See ordering and storage for delivery notes.

When color can be a signal

When experiencing illness, changes in bodily colors (such as yellow, green, red, blue, or purple) can often indicate underlying health conditions or infections. These signals warrant a consultation with a qualified healthcare provider for proper evaluation, rather than relying on unverified remedies or self-diagnoses.

Colors also represent meaningful spiritual connections.

Detoxing and cleansing

Detoxing is about reducing exposure to harmful substances. Cleansing is a short, focused way of eating — often a juice plan — to support digestion and energy. Choose based on your goals, and consult a healthcare provider, especially if you are pregnant, on medication, managing diabetes, or considering this for a child.
